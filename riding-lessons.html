Horseback riding lessons in Brooklyn

Learn to ride — and to truly care for a horse — with patient instructors at Brooklyn's only centrally located stable.

Lessons for children

Kids ride in semi-private lessons that pair riding time with real horsemanship. Beyond the saddle, students learn grooming, tacking up, and ground work, so they build confidence around horses, not just on top of them. Our after-school and summer programs extend this into ongoing, multi-session learning.

Lessons for adults

Adults are welcome from the very first time in the saddle through advanced work. Classes run in partnership with the Manhattan Riding Club, with instruction matched to your level and goals — whether you want a calm weekend hobby or serious skills.

How we teach

  • Small groups and semi-private formats, so you get real attention.
  • Horses treated as partners — riders learn to read and respect them.
  • A focus on safety, seat, and fundamentals before speed.
  • Helmets required and provided; wear closed-toe shoes with a small heel if you have them.

Getting started

Tell us who's riding, their age, and any past experience, and we'll recommend the right class. Email Be@Quadrozzi.com or call (718) 972-4588 to book your first lesson.
