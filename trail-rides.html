Trail rides through historic Prospect Park

See Brooklyn from the saddle on a guided hack along the park's 3.5-mile bridle path — one of the few places in New York City you can still ride through the woods.

The bridle path

Prospect Park's 3.5-mile historic bridle path winds through woodlands, open meadows, and shaded ravines — the landscape Olmsted and Vaux designed to feel far from the city. On a guided ride you cover ground on horseback that most visitors only ever walk.

Who can ride

Guided hacks work for individuals and families looking for a one-of-a-kind urban riding experience. A guide sets the pace and stays with your group throughout. If you're brand new to riding, we'll match you to a steady horse and keep things at a walk; tell us your experience when you book.

Good to know

  • Helmets are required and provided.
  • Wear long pants and closed-toe shoes; a small heel helps in the stirrup.
  • Rides are weather-dependent — we'll reschedule if conditions aren't safe.
  • Meet at the stable, 51-55 Caton Place, Brooklyn.
