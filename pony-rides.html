Pony rides in the heart of Brooklyn

A calm, well-supervised first meeting with horses for toddlers and young children — Sundays at Prospect Park Stable, right in central Brooklyn.

When pony rides run

Our Be • Pony Rider season runs May through October, every Sunday from 1:00 to 3:00 PM, rain or shine. No booking marathon required — families come by during the window and take turns.

Who it's for

Pony rides suit toddlers and young children who are meeting a horse for the first time. A handler leads the pony the whole way while a parent walks alongside, so even the most cautious first-timer stays comfortable. Helmets are provided and required for every rider.

What to expect on the day

  • Check in at the stable at 51-55 Caton Place, Brooklyn.
  • Your child is fitted with a helmet and lifted onto a gentle, experienced pony.
  • A staff member leads a slow ride while you walk beside them.
  • Time to meet the horses and, on many days, watch them being groomed and bathed.

Tickets and pricing

Rides are offered at an affordable, family-friendly rate. Grab Sunday tickets through the Be • Pony Rider ticket shop, or just email us to plan your visit.

Birthday parties & private pony experiences

Want a pony for a birthday or a special occasion? We put together custom "my pony" experiences. Email Be@Quadrozzi.com with your date and we'll help you plan it.
